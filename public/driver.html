Quick guide

Getting around the Driver Portal

How do I check today's route?

Open Today's route in the sidebar. Each stop shows its scheduled time, status, and any notes from dispatch.

Where can I see my salary and payslip?

Go to Salary to see this month's breakdown — base pay, bonuses, allowances, and deductions — plus your payment history for the last 3 months.

How do I find a past trip?

The Trip history tab lists your recent trips with route, distance, and earnings for each one.

My document shows as expired or expiring soon — what do I do?

Check the Documents tab for the exact status. Contact the depot office to renew any document marked warning or expired.
